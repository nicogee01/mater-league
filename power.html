Look beyond the scores

  • Injuries and suspensions. A club missing key starters isn't the same club this week.
  • Transfers. Who they signed and who they let go since last gameweek, on waivers or in trades.
  • Players back from injury. A returning star usually needs a game or two to get up to speed, so don't rank on his name alone.
  • Anything else that matters. Tough fixtures ahead, rotation risk, form that looks too good to last.
